The workflow

A publishing workflow with you in control.

Each step is creator-directed. The software does not automatically gain permission to publish merely because a platform account is connected.

01 / CONNECT

Authorize an account

Review and approve provider-hosted OAuth permissions for the social account you own or manage.

02 / PREPARE

Review a video

Check format, duration and orientation. Choose titles, hashtags, audience designation and visibility.

03 / PUBLISH

Schedule and track

Choose the time and monitor the status of each destination separately, including failures or authorization issues.

Availability depends on the provider, approved app scopes, and account capabilities. This public website does not host the uploader or expose the owner's dashboard.

Responsible by design

No surprise posts.

The creator selects each destination and post. TikTok publishing, if approved, requires a fresh, explicit consent decision and uses the creator's available privacy settings. A post already being processed by a provider might not be cancellable.

Privacy

The public site is informational.

No passwords collected here

This site does not request or store Google, TikTok or other login passwords, Social Publisher API keys, OAuth tokens or uploaded videos.

Your backend, your data

The Social Publisher backend is installed on the operator's own device or server, with local media storage and encrypted OAuth tokens.
