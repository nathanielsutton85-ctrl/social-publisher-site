How Social Publisher works

A quick look at the local publishing workflow and how account permissions, privacy and scheduling fit together.

1. Open the local application

Social Publisher is self-hosted. Its owner dashboard and authenticated API run on a device or server controlled by the operator. This public website is separate: it does not accept uploads or access private app credentials.

2. Connect an account

The owner selects a destination and follows an official provider-hosted authorization link. TikTok and YouTube credentials are never entered into this static website. Provider authorization grants the specific permissions requested and approved.

3. Prepare the video

The creator selects a video they have the right to publish. The application checks details such as file format, duration, resolution, codec and frame rate against platform-specific constraints.

4. Choose publishing settings

The creator sets a title, description, destination, schedule and privacy setting. For YouTube, the creator must specify the made-for-kids audience status. TikTok direct posting, where available, requires the creator to review live privacy settings and explicitly consent to every individual post.

5. Track the status

Scheduled jobs progress through queued, uploading, processing, published or error states. Publication can be delayed or rejected by connectivity, platform restrictions, host availability, rate limits or app-review requirements.

6. Disconnect or remove data

The operator can unlink accounts, remove media and manage local records and backups. Unlinking removes stored active account authorization tokens from the application's account record while completed-post history can remain. Videos already published on a platform must be managed through that provider.

About TikTok integration: Direct posting is subject to TikTok's Content Posting API approval, approved video.publish scope, verified video URL ownership and account-specific posting restrictions. A TikTok connection does not guarantee public posting approval.

Frequently asked questions

Is this website the publisher dashboard?

No. The website provides information and legal policies. The working publisher dashboard is served locally by the installed software, not from this public site.

Does connecting an account automatically publish?

No. The creator still selects individual videos and publishing details, and TikTok direct posts need additional express consent.

Will the Mac publish while asleep or powered off?

No. A self-hosted scheduler requires a running, network-connected host. Unattended operation requires an always-on server.

How do I get support?

Open a public GitHub issue for general, non-sensitive questions. Never post private account data or secrets publicly.
